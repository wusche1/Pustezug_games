Falscher Fisch

A tense one on one, a duel of the mind

Falscher Fisch is a 2 player game that might look like a simple game of chance, but hides a multitude of strategies and tactics.

The two contenders play a series of rounds, picking one of three symbols and forming them with their hands — not unlike rock-paper-scissors.

The Symbols

Water

Form a closed fist and rest it vertically on your other flat hand.

Hook

Form a fist, rest it wrist-up on your flat hand, and extend your curved index finger.

Fish

Form a curved surface with your hand and put it wrist-down on your flat hand.

How to Play

1

Your Hand

Each game you have:

  • 2× Water
  • 3× Hook
  • 1× Fish
2

Your Goal

Play your fish first without getting caught!

3

Outcomes

  • 🐟 Fish vs 💧 Water → You win!
  • 🐟 Fish vs 🪝 Hook → You lose!
  • 🐟 Fish vs 🐟 Fish → Draw, new game!
  • Any other combo → Nothing, next round!

The Ritual

When counting down to your move, both players rhythmically beat their fist into their flat hand, emphatically calling out:

"Fal-scher Fisch!"

Beat your hand with every syllable. When enchanting the magical word "Fisch", form the symbol you want to play.
