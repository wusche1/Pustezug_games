J'accuse!

Convince the group, encrypt your knowledge

Play Now Word Library

J'accuse! is a social deduction game where you don't know whether you should hunt or hide.

Play with 5-10 friends, accuse them of being in the minority team, or sneak around and steal their information.

The Setup

1

Two Words

Each player receives a secret word. There are only 2 different words in the game — a majority word and a minority word.

2

Hidden Identity

When you get your word, you don't know if it's the majority or minority word. You must figure it out!

3

Your Goal

Majority: Identify and condemn all minority members.
Minority: Discover the majority word.

On Your Turn

Ask a Question

Ask any player a question to reveal information about their word. Questions can be about anything, and answers don't have to be truthful!

No insider information — don't reference private knowledge between you and that player.

Accuse Someone

Point your finger and trigger a vote! All non-condemned players vote yes or no. If more than half vote yes, the accused is condemned.

Condemned players can't talk, can't be asked questions, and skip their turns.

The Buzzer

At any point (while not condemned), you can shout:

"BUZZER!"

You then have one minute to guess 3 words. If the majority word is among your guesses, the minority wins!

Only use the Buzzer if you're confident you're in the minority!

How the Game Ends

Majority Wins

All minority members are condemned

Minority Wins

  • Two majority members are condemned, OR
  • The majority word is guessed via Buzzer

Pro Tips

The best word pairs are somewhat related but not too similar — like "The Taj Mahal" and "The Kremlin" (both famous landmarks, different places).
